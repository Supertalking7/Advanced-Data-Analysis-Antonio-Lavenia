About Us

This is a dynamic Airbnb pricing application developed by Antonio Lavenia, a student in the Advanced Data Analysis course at the University of Lausanne.

The application uses machine learning algorithms trained on real Airbnb data from Sicily to provide accurate price recommendations. Our model considers multiple factors including property characteristics, seasonal patterns, location premium, and market demand to suggest optimal pricing strategies.

Built with modern web technologies and powered by advanced data science techniques, this tool helps Airbnb hosts maximize their revenue while maintaining competitive pricing in the Sicilian market.
